# University Internal IT Helpdesk System

## Overview

This project focuses on the analysis and design of an Internal IT Helpdesk System for a university, aiming to improve IT service management, streamline support processes, and enhance the overall experience of students, academic staff, and administrative employees.

The project examines the existing IT support environment, identifies operational challenges, and proposes a centralized helpdesk solution featuring ticket management, request tracking, knowledge base management, automated workflows, and performance monitoring.

## Academic Context

This project was developed as part of the **IT System Analytics** course at **[Vilnius University](https://www.vu.lt/en)**, within the **Erasmus+ exchange programme**.

The project was conducted under the academic supervision of **Lina Druskiene**, course instructor at Vilnius University.

## Project Objectives

* Analyze the current IT support processes and identify areas for improvement.
* Evaluate stakeholder needs, roles, and responsibilities.
* Design a centralized IT Helpdesk solution aligned with the university's digital transformation strategy.
* Define functional, non-functional, technical, and legal requirements.
* Assess potential business benefits, risks, and implementation considerations.
* Establish validation criteria and test scenarios for key system requirements.

## Key Areas of Analysis

The project incorporates several business analysis and strategic management frameworks:

* **PESTLE Analysis** – Evaluation of external factors affecting IT service management.
* **VMOST Analysis** – Alignment of the proposed solution with organizational strategy.
* **Stakeholder Analysis** – Identification and assessment of key stakeholders.
* **CATWOE Analysis** – Understanding different stakeholder perspectives.
* **Business Activity Model (BAM)** – Mapping essential business activities.
* **McKinsey 7S Framework** – Assessment of organizational readiness.
* **POPIT Analysis** – Evaluation of People, Organization, Processes, Information, and Technology.
* **Gap Analysis** – Identification of differences between the current and desired state.
* **RAID Analysis** – Identification of risks, assumptions, issues, and dependencies.
* **Requirements Management** – Definition and validation of system requirements.

## Proposed Solution

The proposed Internal IT Helpdesk System aims to introduce a centralized platform that supports:

* Centralized ticket creation and management.
* Incident categorization, prioritization, and assignment.
* Real-time request tracking and status updates.
* Automated notifications and workflow management.
* Service Level Agreement (SLA) monitoring.
* Knowledge base and self-service capabilities.
* Performance monitoring and reporting.

## Expected Benefits

* Improved IT support efficiency and response times.
* Greater transparency in request handling.
* Reduced duplicate and unresolved support requests.
* Enhanced user satisfaction.
* Better resource allocation through data-driven insights.
* Improved knowledge sharing and process standardization.
